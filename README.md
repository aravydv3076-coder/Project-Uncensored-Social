An Urgent Appeal to Developers & the Tech Community: Our Country is Burning and We Need Your Help!
The truth is being actively suppressed, and our voices and ground-reality news are being deleted from mainstream social media platforms. To bypass this heavy censorship, we urgently need an application or platform that no government can easily ban, take down, or wipe from our devices.
We are common people, not tech experts. We need an app that is as simple to use as mainstream social media but comes with these features in-built:
 * In-built VPN/Tor: People shouldn't have to download external VPNs when websites are blocked. The app should have a simple toggle to connect to open VPNs or the Tor network instantly.
 * Private DNS: Built-in encrypted DNS so ISPs cannot track what users are posting or viewing.
 * Encrypted Vault (Anti-Deletion): If authorities physically check phones to delete evidence/videos, the app must have a hidden, password-protected vault that is impossible to access or wipe without the user's consent.
 * Viral Offline Sync (P2P/Mesh Network): If the government completely shuts down the internet and tries to wipe data in a specific area, the app should use Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth to instantly and automatically sync the saved news/videos to all nearby users. If even one person has the real data, it should spread locally like wildfire without needing the internet.
 * Decentralized System: It shouldn't rely on a single central server that a government can just pull the plug on.
The most crucial point: The masses do not know how to configure DNS, set up VPNs, or use complex mesh networks. All these anti-censorship tools must work seamlessly in the background as basic toggle options. A normal user should just open the app and be able to see and share the truth safely.
Funding: If you need resources to build, host, and maintain this project, we are completely ready for crowdfunding.
